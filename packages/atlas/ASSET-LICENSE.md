metadata, and models remain separate data artifacts and are not relicensed by
the package.

[Z-Anatomy](https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models), credited to Gauthier Kervyn,
Marcin Zielinski, additional Z-Anatomy contributors, and the upstream sources
documented by that project. Z-Anatomy-derived material is subject to
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

When redistributing a self-hosted atlas, preserve its catalog provenance and
attribution, identify modifications, link to the license, and follow the
applicable ShareAlike terms. The upstream licenses are authoritative.
